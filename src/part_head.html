Game setup

How this works
Fill in the game details and press START GAME. Log every action with one tap on a player's card — the score, box score and play-by-play build themselves. Opponent shots and goals are logged on the opponent panel and credit the keeper selected under In goal. When the final whistle blows, press END GAME: the game is archived in this browser and a complete Excel workbook downloads automatically. Load those workbooks on the SEASON tab (any computer) to build season totals, tables and charts. Everything runs offline in this one file.

Roster

How this works
Add every cadet once — number, name, position (GK marks goalkeepers). The roster is saved in this browser and reused every game. Untick Dressed for anyone not playing today and they stay off the game screen. Edits here are live; click a cell to change it.
