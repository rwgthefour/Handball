Game setup ▾

How this works
Fill in the game details and press START GAME. Log every action with one tap on a player's card — the score, box score and play-by-play build themselves. Opponent shots and goals are logged on the opponent panel and credit the keeper selected under In goal. When the final whistle blows, press END GAME: the game is archived in this browser and a complete Excel workbook downloads automatically. Load those workbooks on the SEASON tab (any computer) to build season totals, tables and charts. Everything runs offline in this one file.

Stat roster — jersey numbers & dressed list ▸

How this works
This list is what the GAME page tracks against. Pick each cadet from the team cards so their game stats attach to their profile — position and class year fill themselves in (C1C 2027, C2C 2028, C3C 2029, C4C 2030), and you only add the jersey number. Anyone without a card can still be added by name. Leave the # blank and the next free jersey number is assigned for you, counting up from 1. Add every cadet once — number, name, position (GK marks goalkeepers). It is saved in this browser and reused every game; if the team repo carries a roster file it loads automatically the first time. Untick Dressed for anyone not playing today and they stay off the game screen. Edits here are live; click a cell to change it.

About the team

Team handball at the United States Air Force Academy is a cadet club sport. The roster is drawn from across the Cadet Wing — every squadron and every class year — and the team trains and travels together through the season, competing against collegiate programmes from around the country.

The game is seven against seven: six court players and a goalkeeper, two thirty-minute halves, a clock that keeps running. Attackers work the ball around a six-metre arc and throw at a goal two metres tall and three wide, and a seven-metre throw is the penalty. It is fast, physical and continuous — one of the best sports there is to watch from the sideline.

This site is where the season lives. Every game is tracked live — every shot, save, seven-metre and suspension — and those numbers attach to each cadet's profile, so a player's season is one card rather than a stack of spreadsheets. The workbook from every game is published here and the season tables and charts build themselves from it.
